# User Research

Who uses USAJobs and what they are trying to do.

## User groups

Federal job seekers are not a monolith. The service is used by people with significantly different levels of experience, digital literacy, and institutional familiarity.

Key segments to research:

- First-time federal applicants with no prior context for federal hiring processes
- Career changers moving from private sector
- Veterans using preference eligibility
- Current federal employees seeking transfers or promotions
- Non-native English speakers

## Research questions

1. What do users understand about the federal hiring process before they start?
2. Where do users drop off or give up on a listing?
3. What information do users need to decide whether to apply?
4. What makes a job listing trustworthy or credible?
